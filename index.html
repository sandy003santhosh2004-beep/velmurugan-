Our Story

A Legacy of Purity & Trust

Welcome to VELMURUGAN Oil Mills — a legacy of purity and trust since 1909. Founded by the visionary K. Velayutham Chettiar, our company has been dedicated to producing high-quality oils for over a century. We specialize in manufacturing Gingelly Oil, Groundnut Oil, and Coconut Oil at our state-of-the-art facility in Kovilpatti, ensuring every drop meets the highest standards of purity.

In 1949, we proudly secured our trademark, and by 1951, our commitment to excellence was recognized with the prestigious Agmark Certification — setting us apart as a trusted name in the industry. Today, under the visionary leadership of Director M. Kottiyappan and Managing Director K. Badri Narayanan, who took the helm in 2021, we continue to uphold our rich heritage while embracing modern innovations to deliver the finest oils to your home.

At VELMURUGAN Oil Mills, quality is not just a promise — it is our tradition, passed down through generations with unwavering dedication to excellence and the health of every family we serve.
